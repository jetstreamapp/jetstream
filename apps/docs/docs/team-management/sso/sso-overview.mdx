Before configuring an identity provider for your Jetstream team, ensure that you have [verified your organization's domain](./domain-verification.mdx) ownership.

After your domain is verified, you can proceed to set up Single Sign-On (SSO) for your Jetstream team using one of the supported identity providers or a custom OIDC or SAML provider.

Refer to the following guides to configure SSO with your chosen identity provider:

- [Azure AD](./sso-azure.mdx)
- [Google](./sso-google.mdx)
- [Okta](./sso-okta.mdx)
- [Other OIDC or SAML Providers](./sso-generic.mdx)

## Signing up with a verified domain

Once SSO is turned on, nobody with an email address on one of your verified domains can sign up with an email address and password, and nobody can change an existing account's email address to one on those domains.
To join your team they sign in with SSO, which requires either **Auto-create users on first SSO login (JIT Provisioning)** to be enabled or an invitation to your team.

People you invite follow your team's SSO bypass settings for the role they were invited with.
An invitation from a different Jetstream team does not let someone sign up with a password on your domains.

Signing up with Google or Salesforce is not restricted.
Someone can still create a separate Jetstream account by signing in with Google or Salesforce using an address on your domains.
That account is not part of your team, and your team's login settings do not apply to it.
