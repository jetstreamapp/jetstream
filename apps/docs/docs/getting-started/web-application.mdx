The web application is the quickest way to use Jetstream. There is nothing to install, and it is available on every plan,
including the free plan.

## Getting started

1. [Sign up](https://getjetstream.app/auth/signup/) or [log in](https://getjetstream.app/auth/login/) to Jetstream.
2. Click **+ Add Org** at the top of the page and log in to Salesforce to connect your org. See
   [Adding your first org](/#adding-your-first-org) for the details, including the permissions Jetstream asks for.
3. Choose a tool from the navigation bar or the home page.

You can connect as many Salesforce orgs as you like and switch between them from the org dropdown. Use
[org groups](./org-groups.mdx) to keep orgs for different companies or projects apart.

## How the web application connects to Salesforce

The web application connects to your orgs with OAuth 2.0, so Jetstream never sees your Salesforce password. Requests to Salesforce
are sent through Jetstream's servers, and the tokens used to reach your orgs are encrypted before they are stored. See
[Security](./security.mdx) and [Salesforce Data Access](./salesforce-data-access.mdx) for more detail.

If your organization does not want requests to pass through Jetstream's servers, the
[desktop application](./desktop-app.mdx), [browser extension](./browser-extension/browser-extension.mdx) and
[Salesforce managed package](./managed-package/managed-package.mdx) all communicate with Salesforce directly from your computer.

## Choosing a platform

Jetstream is available in four forms, and you can use more than one. They share most of the same tools.

|                                | Web application    | [Desktop application](./desktop-app.mdx) | [Browser extension](./browser-extension/browser-extension.mdx) | [Salesforce managed package](./managed-package/managed-package.mdx) |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Plan                           | All plans          | Paid plans                               | Paid plans                                                     | Team and Enterprise                                                 |
| Where it runs                  | Any modern browser | macOS and Windows                        | Chrome and Firefox                                             | Inside Salesforce                                                   |
| Installation                   | None               | Installed on your computer               | Installed in your browser                                      | Installed in your Salesforce org by an administrator                |
| How orgs are connected         | Add each org       | Add each org                             | Uses the Salesforce org you are logged in to                   | Uses the org it is installed in                                     |
| Work with more than one org    | Yes                | Yes                                      | One org at a time                                              | One org at a time                                                   |
| Requests to Salesforce         | Through Jetstream  | Directly from your computer              | Directly from your browser                                     | Directly from your browser                                          |
| Users need a Jetstream account | Yes                | Yes                                      | Yes                                                            | No                                                                  |

Each platform's page lists the features that are not yet available on it.
