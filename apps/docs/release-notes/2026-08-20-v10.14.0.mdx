## What's new

### Data History

Jetstream keeps a local history of the data modifications you make: what you changed, when, and what
Salesforce returned. Open it from the clock icon in the header, the Load Records menu, or the home
page.

- History is captured for Load Records (including retries and trial runs), Load Records to Multiple
  Objects, Update Records Without a File, Bulk Update From Query, query result edits, and record
  saves from the record modal.
- Each entry records the org, feature, object, operation, status, and record counts. The detail view
  can preview or download the records you submitted, what was sent to Salesforce, and the per-record
  success and failure results as CSV, Excel, or JSON.
- Free plans keep the 15 most recent entries for up to 60 days. Paid plans, the desktop app, and the
  browser extension keep unlimited entries for up to a year. Pin an entry to exclude it from
  automatic cleanup.
- Settings has controls to turn Data History off, adjust how long entries are kept, see how much
  storage it is using, and delete everything saved on the device. On Chrome, Edge, and the desktop
  app you can also store history in a folder on your computer, where the files are visible, backed
  up, and survive clearing browser data.

History is stored on your device and is never sent to the Jetstream server. That also means clearing
your browser's site data deletes it, so download anything you need to keep. Loading Custom Metadata
records is not captured, since those loads go through the Metadata API rather than the data APIs.

### Refresh All Orgs

The org list has a **Refresh All Orgs** button that checks every org connection and reports how many
succeeded. Salesforce ends a connection when an org has not been used for 30 days, so this also
resets that clock across all of your orgs. Orgs that fail the check are called out so you know which
ones to reconnect.

Refreshing a single org from its card now updates the card when the check fails. Previously a failed
refresh left the card showing its old status until you reloaded the page.

### Other fixes

- Query: downloads of 500,000 or more records now use the Bulk API. They previously stayed on the
  standard path and failed partway through building the file.
- Query: switching objects while a field list was still loading could show the fields of the object
  you switched away from, or leave a spinner that never finished. Field loads are now tied to the
  object that requested them.
