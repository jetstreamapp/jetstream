## What's new

### Email logo fix for Gmail

Since 10.21.0, the Jetstream logo in emails from Jetstream showed up as a black box when the email was opened in Gmail. Emails sent from now on show the logo correctly. Emails you already received recover within about a day, once Gmail refreshes its copy of the image.
